Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Keeperpass is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our password manager service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Sign up for an account using Google authentication
  • Store passwords and sensitive data in your vault
  • Contact us for support
  • Use our application features

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Store your encrypted vault securely
  • Authenticate your access to your account
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our service

4. End-to-End Encryption

All your passwords and sensitive data are encrypted locally on your device using AES-256-GCM encryption before being transmitted to our servers. Keeperpass servers store only encrypted data. We cannot access, view, or decrypt your passwords or vault contents under any circumstances.

5. Data Storage

Your encrypted vault is stored securely in Google Drive. We do not store unencrypted passwords on our servers. Your master secret is never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

6. Third-Party Services

We use Google Drive to store your encrypted vault. Google Drive's privacy policy governs the storage and handling of your encrypted data. We also use Google Authentication for sign-in purposes. Please review Google's privacy policy for more information about their data practices.

7. Security Audit Features

Keeperpass includes a Security Report feature that scans your passwords for weaknesses. This feature:

  • Performs weak password detection locally on your device
  • Checks for password reuse within your vault
  • Queries the Have I Been Pwned API only for compromised password checks
  • Only sends 5-character hash prefixes to the HIBP API (your full password is never sent)

8. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

9. Data Retention

Your encrypted vault persists in Google Drive until you delete your account or reset your vault. We retain only the encrypted data necessary to provide the service.

  • Access your account and data at any time
  • Delete your vault and associated data
  • Request information about what data we hold
  • Disconnect your account from Google Drive
