4. Your master secret, and what we cannot do

Your vault is encrypted with a key derived from your master secret. That secret is never transmitted to us and is never stored anywhere outside your device. This has consequences you must accept before relying on the Service:

  • We cannot recover your vault. If you forget your master secret, the contents of your vault cannot be decrypted by us, by Google, or by anyone else. The data is not locked away pending a support request — it is mathematically unavailable.
  • There is no password reset in the usual sense. Resetting your secret does not recover anything. It moves your existing encrypted file aside, keeping it as a timestamped backup in your Drive in case you later remember the original secret, and starts a fresh, empty vault.
  • Keeping your own copies is your responsibility. The app provides an export feature. We strongly recommend using it to keep your own backups, stored securely.
  • Your Google account security is part of your vault's security. Anyone with access to both your Google session and your master secret can open your vault.

You accept sole responsibility for safeguarding your master secret and your Google account credentials.
